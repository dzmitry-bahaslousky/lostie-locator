# Lostie Locator: Moderation plan

Goal: keep reports trustworthy and protect users from spam, scams and fake
"found" reports, without slowing down people who urgently need help.

## First version

1. **Report button** on every post, chat and profile. A post with 3 reports
   from different users is hidden until a moderator reviews it.
2. **Admin review queue** where a moderator can approve, remove or ban.
3. **New-account limits:** verified email or phone, and at most 2 posts per
   day during an account's first week.
4. **Automatic checks:** no links or phone numbers in public posts (contact
   happens in in-app chat), and posts reusing a photo already on the site are
   flagged for review.
5. **Scam protection in chat:** a warning never to pay a "finder" before seeing
   the pet; both sides can block and report.

## Later

- AI image checks (stock photos, non-pet images).
- Reputation score for helpers.
- Community moderators.
